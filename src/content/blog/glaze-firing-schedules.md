## Why one kiln for two cones gets messy

If your members work in both mid-range and high-fire clay bodies, you already know the argument: a half-full cone 10 load sits for two weeks because nobody wants to fire it empty, and the cone 6 people keep jumping the queue.

We asked three studios how they handle it.

## Fixed days

Hollow Pine Clay in Asheville fires cone 6 on Mondays and Thursdays and cone 10 on the first and third Saturday. A load fires on its day whether it is full or not. Members plan around it, and the owner says complaints dropped to almost nothing once the calendar was posted.

## Fill threshold

Mudroom Ceramics fires a load once it reaches 80% of the shelf space, whichever cone it is. Kilnbook shows the fill level for each queue, so members can see when their load is close.

## Hybrid

Fireweed Studio uses fixed days for cone 6 and a fill threshold for cone 10, because high-fire members are fewer and more patient.

## What we built from this

Each kiln in Kilnbook can now have a schedule rule per cone: a fixed day, a fill threshold, or both. Members see the next expected firing date on their piece, which answers the question before they ask it.

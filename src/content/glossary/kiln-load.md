A kiln load is one firing's worth of work. Loading means arranging pieces on shelves held up by posts, building up layer by layer until the kiln is full.

How much fits depends on the firing. A [bisque load](/glossary/bisque-firing) can be packed tightly, with pieces touching and nesting. A [glaze load](/glossary/glaze-firing) needs space around every piece, so it holds noticeably less.

## When to fire a load

Every studio has to decide when a load is ready to go. The common answers are:

- **Fixed days**: the kiln fires on set days whether it is full or not.
- **Fill threshold**: the kiln fires once the load reaches a set level, for example 80% of shelf space.
- **A mix**: fixed days for the busy queue, a fill threshold for the quieter one.

Firing half-empty loads costs electricity or gas and kiln wear. Waiting for a full load keeps members waiting. Whatever rule you choose, posting it where members can see it heads off most of the complaints.

## Kiln loads in Kilnbook

Kilnbook shows how full each load is, lets you move pieces between loads and mark a load as fired with one tap. When a load is unloaded, every member with work in it gets a text. [How firing queues work](/features/firings).
